Flight controls
You have full control in manual mode

↑ / W pushes the nose down · ↓ / S pulls the nose up
Hold a key to increase the angle. Release to keep that attitude. Click Climb / Descend for a 5° adjustment. Level explicitly stops the climb or descent. Dive points the nose down 65°.

← / A and → / D turn · E / Q change power
L levels · P pauses · C changes camera · I shows or hides the panel · hold B to brake. Z / X control rudder. Use the Nose target slider for fine landing adjustments. Reduce speed on final, ease the descent before touchdown and brake on the runway.

Protection is OFF by default: terrain impacts end the flight. You can enable it above. Assisted mode holds altitude when controls are released. The AI pilot always uses protection.

The green marker shows the flight path; the yellow symbol shows the nose. Watch ALTITUDE, VERTICAL and HEIGHT AGL.

Manual attitude control.

Gamepad: pull back to raise the nose, push forward to lower it. Z / X: rudder · hold B: brakes.

FLIGHT AND PRACTICE

Raise the nose to rotate at take-off speed. Reduce power and brake after touchdown. Approach guidance is available when aligned with the runway.
