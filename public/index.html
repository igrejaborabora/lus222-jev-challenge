01 / THE PIXELGRAMMAR USE CASE

Real-time simulation.
Built to test AI under pressure.

Pixelgrammar designs and engineers interactive 3D systems, mission-critical interfaces and applied AI architectures. In this use case, we built a full WebGL flight deck over Porto where a human pilot and TypeSafe AI’s JEV share the exact same aircraft physics, instruments and safety supervisor.

01

Fly the LUS-222 manually.

Take direct control of throttle, pitch and roll with real-time PFD instruments, twin-engine telemetry and optional envelope protection.

02

Execute the LPPR approach.

Start on final over Porto, align with the runway threshold, manage sink rate and inspect touchdown telemetry afterwards.

03

Put JEV in command.

Assign JEV a route or landing objective. Watch its typed choices, calibrated confidence and millisecond latency live—and take over anytime.

02 / WHAT IS JEV & WHY IT MATTERS

No chat prose.
Typed decisions in milliseconds.

JEV (typesafe-ai/jev) is a frontier evaluation model accessed via experimental_evaluate. Unlike conversational LLMs that stream free-form text, JEV ingests structured telemetry and returns schema-locked Choice, Score and Boolean decisions with calibrated probability distributions in a single fast call.
